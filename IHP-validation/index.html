Model and source

Palace sweep: 1-50 GHz, 11 points; authoritative IHP SG13G2 stack, TopMetal2 winding, TopMetal1 feeds/underpass, SUBGND reference plane, 5 µm refined / 70 µm maximum Palace mesh, interlayer ports SUBGND to TopMetal1 at 50 Ohm. OpenEMS was not available; pinned measured S-parameters are the approved fallback. The IHP OpenEMS reference uses SG13_L2n0_2port_groundpolygon_mesh1.5um.py with 1.5 µm refined cells, lambda_air/(sqrt(eps_max)*20) maximum cells, PEC boundaries and 0-30 GHz/401 points.
