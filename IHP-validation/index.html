Model and source

Palace sweep: 1-50 GHz, 11 points; authoritative IHP SG13G2 stack with a 280 µm substrate, TopMetal2 winding, TopMetal1 feeds/underpass, and a minimal ground pad with PEC boundaries for the best variant. The 5 µm refined / 70 µm maximum mesh and Palace Conductivity+Thickness surface-impedance BCs (sigma=3.77e7 S/m; delta ~2.6 um at 1 GHz) model finite conductors. OpenEMS was not available; pinned measured S-parameters are the approved fallback.

The IHP OpenEMS reference uses SG13_L2n0_2port_groundpolygon_mesh1.5um.py with TopMetal1 edge ports, a central Metal1->TopMetal1 PEC pillar, Metal1 ground box, 1.5 µm refined cells, lambda_air/(sqrt(eps_max)*20) maximum cells, PEC boundaries and 0-30 GHz/401 points. The `openems_ground` variant approximates this with a Metal1 rectangle and vertical via ports; it omits the central pillar.
